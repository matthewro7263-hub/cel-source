Liquid Glass Kit

Hubs Spokes Reserves Assets Chains

Move across the panel. The lens bends the live DOM layer underneath it.

This demo recreates the Aave-style glass treatment for the web: a real HTML layer remains selectable and interactive, while a matching layer inside the lens is displaced, split slightly by color channel, and topped with a soft rim highlight.

The effect works as a component pattern rather than a static image, so it can sit on switches, sliders, segmented controls, QR canvases, and video controls.
